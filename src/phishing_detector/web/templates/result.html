I already opened this link

This check looked at the page from the outside. It cannot see what happened on your device, so start from what you did.

I only opened the page

Close it if you do not trust it. Do not enter anything and do not accept a download. Opening a page on its own does not tell us your device was affected.

I entered a password or a security code

Go to the real service yourself, using its own app or an address you type from memory, and change that password. Check the active sessions on the account and sign the others out. If you use that password anywhere else, change it there too.

I entered card or bank details

Call your bank or card provider now, through its app or the number printed on the card. Tell them what you entered and do what they say.

I downloaded or opened a file

Do not open it again. Run whatever security scan your device has. If it is a work machine, tell your IT team what happened.
